# B1 in 60 days: plan

**Start:** Tag 1 comes after the baseline from 14 November 2026. **Goal:** pass a full B1 mock exam with at least 60 % in every module by Tag 59–60, so the real Goethe B1 can be booked for late January or February 2027.

**Time each day:** 60–75 minutes. Every day has all four skills. Keep going even when a day goes badly; doing it every day matters more than perfect days.

## The B1 exam (Goethe-Zertifikat B1)
Each module is passed separately. You need 60 % in each one.

| Module | Time | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| **Lesen** | 65 min | T1 blog/personal text (true/false) · T2 two press articles (a/b/c) · T3 match ads to situations · T4 opinions (yes/no) · T5 rules or instructions (a/b/c) |
| **Hören** | about 40 min | T1 five short texts (heard twice) · T2 a talk or tour (once) · T3 everyday conversation (once) · T4 radio discussion: who says what (twice) |
| **Schreiben** | 60 min | T1 informal email, **80 words** · T2 forum post giving an opinion, **80 words** · T3 formal message, **40 words** |
| **Sprechen** | 15 min, in pairs | T1 plan something together · T2 presentation with 5 slides · T3 react to feedback and answer questions |
