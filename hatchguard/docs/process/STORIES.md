# My interview stories

One per sprint. Four to six sentences. Always with a number. Say "I", not "we".

## Template
**Situation.** (where, what was the problem)
**Task.** (what was mine to do)
**Action.** (what I did, and one decision with its reason)
**Result.** (a number, and what I would do differently)
